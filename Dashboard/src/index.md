# US Domestic Aviation (2015–2025)

**Team 8: Gacha**

An interactive exploration of US domestic aviation,
including passenger traffic, airport rankings,
recovery, connectivity, and operational performance.

## Research Questions

1. [Airport and Carrier Rankings](./q1)
2. Aviation Recovery
3. Traffic vs Population
4. Airport Connectivity
5. Airport Groups
6. On-Time Performance
7. Capacity vs Connectivity

## Project Information

**Data:** Bureau of Transportation Statistics and US Census Bureau

**Tools:** Python, D3.js, Observable Framework

**Team Members:** Ronak Pradhan and Joe Perinchery
